Trending #2

AI Action Figure

The action figure trend seals you inside a toy box — clear plastic front, cardboard backing, and tiny accessories that tell your story. It became the most shared AI photo format of the year. Here is the exact recipe.

What you need

A full or half-body photo of yourself and a free AI image editor such as Google Gemini (Nano Banana). The clearer the photo, the better the figure looks like you.

Copy-paste prompts

Prompt 1 — Classic starter pack

Turn the person in this photo into a collectible action figure inside a toy box: clear plastic front, cardboard backing, the figure wearing the same outfit, with small accessories beside them that match their life — phone, headphones, coffee cup, sneakers. Bright studio product photo, keep the face identical.

Prompt 2 — Professional version

Create an action figure of the person in this photo, sealed in retail toy packaging. Same clothes and face. Add miniature accessories: laptop, notebook, handbag, watch. Clean bright background, realistic plastic shine, product photography style.

Prompt 3 — Gamer / creator version

Make the person in this photo into a boxed action figure with accessories: game controller, microphone, camera, cap. Toy-store packaging with a clear plastic window, figure in the same pose and outfit, face unchanged, ultra detailed.

Step by step

  1. Choose a sharp photo — full body works best; half body also works.
  2. Upload it to your AI image app.
  3. Paste a prompt and swap the accessories for things that actually represent you — that's what makes people share it.
  4. Refine once: “make the plastic clearer” or “add [item] as an accessory”.
  5. Download full size and post it with your real photo side by side.

Make yours stand out

  • Accessories are the whole joke — pick 3–4 items only people who know you would get.
  • Professions do brilliantly: chef figures with tiny pans, drivers with tiny cars, gym figures with tiny dumbbells.
  • If the face drifts, regenerate rather than accepting a stranger's face.
