How Big Will GTA 6 Be?

“GTA 6 size” is one of the most searched gaming questions in the world right now — everyone wants to know if their console has room. Here is the honest answer, split into what is confirmed and what is still rumor.

How big will GTA 6 be?

Short answer: Rockstar has not announced the official install size yet. Based on the size of Rockstar's recent games and the scale of GTA 6's world, most expectations land between 150 GB and 200 GB — but treat any exact number you see online as an estimate until Rockstar or the console stores publish the real one.

Confirmed facts: GTA 5 today takes roughly 110 GB with all updates. Red Dead Redemption 2 is about 120 GB. GTA 6 is currently scheduled for release on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its map (Vice City and the state of Leonida) is far larger than GTA 5's Los Santos.

Still rumor: The “200 GB” and “300 GB” figures in viral posts are guesses, not leaks from Rockstar. File size also depends on final compression — studios often shrink the download size in the last months before launch.

Why are modern games so big?

Three things: 4K textures (every wall, car and face is stored in much higher detail), bigger worlds (more buildings you can enter means more data), and audio — hundreds of hours of voices and music in many languages. GTA 6 is extreme on all three.

Will it fit on a PS5 or Xbox?

Yes — but it will be the biggest single guest on your drive. A base PS5 has about 667 GB of usable space, so a 150–200 GB install is roughly a quarter to a third of the whole console. Many players will delete one or two big games before launch day. An external SSD must be the right type (NVMe for PS5) to play PS5 games from it.

Can I pre-load GTA 6?

Pre-loading is standard for Rockstar launches on both consoles, usually opening a few days before release — but the exact date will only be confirmed closer to launch. If your internet is slow, pre-loading is the difference between playing on day one and watching a progress bar.

When will the real size be known?

The exact install size normally appears on the PlayStation and Xbox store pages a few weeks before release, when pre-loads open. We will update this page the day the official number is published.
