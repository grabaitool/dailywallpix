AI Photo Prompts Library

Every prompt on this page is free to copy. Paste one into Google Gemini (Nano Banana), ChatGPT or any AI photo editor along with your photo — and always add “keep my face exactly the same” if the result changes you too much.

Trending Photo Styles

AI Polaroid

Turn this photo into a realistic instant Polaroid picture: white frame, slightly faded film colors, soft flash look, candid 90s party photo feeling. Keep the person exactly the same.

AI Action Figure

Turn the person in this photo into a collectible action figure inside a toy box package, with miniature accessories that match their personality, studio product lighting, keep the face clearly recognizable. Do not add readable brand text.

AI Ghostface

Add the Ghostface character from the Scream movies standing silently in the doorway behind me, 90s horror movie still, direct flash photography, moody and realistic. Keep me exactly the same, do not change my face.

AI Barbie Doll

Restyle this portrait in a Barbie doll aesthetic: glossy doll-like finish, glamorous makeup, pink dreamhouse background, soft studio light, fashionable pink outfit. Keep my face clearly recognizable.

AI Saree Look

Restyle this portrait as a 90s Bollywood film still: the woman wearing an elegant deep-red saree with gold jewelry, soft film grain, warm dramatic lighting, romantic retro mood. Keep her face exactly the same.

Hug My Younger Self

Create a heartwarming photo of me as I am now warmly hugging a young child version of myself — the same face as a 7-year-old, same features. Soft window light, tender and realistic, like a real family photograph.
