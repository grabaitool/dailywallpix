Trending #5

AI Saree Look

The saree trend restyles any portrait as a 90s Bollywood film still — an elegant saree, gold jewelry, soft film grain and warm, dramatic light. It crossed the whole internet in a matter of weeks.

What you need

Any clear portrait and a free AI image editor like Google Gemini (Nano Banana) — this is the trend that made that model famous.

Copy-paste prompts

Prompt 1 — Classic red

Restyle this portrait as a 90s Bollywood film still: the woman wearing an elegant deep-red saree with gold jewelry, soft film grain, warm dramatic lighting, romantic retro mood. Keep her face exactly the same.

Prompt 2 — Yellow in the fields

Turn this photo into a retro Bollywood scene: yellow saree flowing in the wind, sunflower field background, golden-hour light, 90s film grain and colors. Same face, joyful expression.

Prompt 3 — Monsoon mood

Make this portrait a moody 90s Bollywood monsoon still: dark blue saree, rain-kissed hair, soft window light, film grain, cinematic and emotional. Keep the face unchanged.

Step by step

  1. Pick a portrait with your face clear and looking at or near the camera.
  2. Paste a prompt, choose your saree color story — red is classic, yellow is joyful, blue is moody.
  3. Add grain if it looks too clean — “more film grain, slightly faded colors” sells the 90s feel.
