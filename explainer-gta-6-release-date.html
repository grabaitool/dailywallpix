When Is GTA 6 Coming Out?

“When is GTA 6 coming out?” has been one of the most searched questions in gaming for two years — partly because the answer kept moving. Here is the current, confirmed date, and the short story of how it got there.

Confirmed: GTA 6 is scheduled to release on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That date comes from Rockstar Games' own announcement — it is the date to plan around.

Why do some sites still say May 2026?

Because that was the previous date. GTA 6 was first aimed at late 2025, then officially moved to May 26, 2026, and then delayed once more to November 19, 2026. Older articles and videos were never updated — if a page says May 2026, it is out of date.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC at launch?

Not announced. Rockstar has only confirmed PS5 and Xbox Series X|S for launch. With GTA 5, the PC version arrived later — many players expect the same pattern here, but no PC date exists yet, and anything you see claiming one is speculation.

What about PS4 and Xbox One?

No — GTA 6 is built for the current generation only. It will not release on PS4 or Xbox One.

When can I pre-order and pre-load?

Pre-orders are typically live on the PlayStation and Xbox stores well before launch, and pre-loads usually open a few days before release day. Exact timings are announced closer to launch — we will update this page when they are.

Rumor check: “Rockstar will delay it again” is a popular guess, not news. Studios delay games to finish them, and Rockstar has a long history of doing exactly that — but as of now, November 19, 2026 is the official date, and no newer announcement exists.
