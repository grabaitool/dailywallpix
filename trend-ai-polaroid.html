Trending #1

AI Polaroid Photos

The AI Polaroid look turns any photo into a warm, flash-lit instant print — thick white border, slightly faded colors, that unmistakable 90s feeling. It ranked among the biggest internet photo trends of the year. Here is how to make yours in about a minute.

What you need

Any photo of you (or you and friends), and a free AI image app that can edit photos — Google Gemini (the Nano Banana image model) is the one most people use for this trend. No design skills needed.

Copy-paste prompts

Upload your photo, paste one of these, and generate. Adjust a word or two to fit your picture.

Prompt 1 — Classic Polaroid

Turn this photo into a nostalgic Polaroid-style instant photo: direct flash look, slightly faded warm colors, soft grain, thick white border at the bottom, candid 90s party-photo feeling. Keep the people exactly the same.

Prompt 2 — Beach / holiday version

Restyle this photo as a vintage instant-camera print from a 90s beach holiday: bright flash, sun-faded colors, white Polaroid border, relaxed candid mood, a little film grain.

Prompt 3 — Birthday / party version

Make this photo look like a Polaroid taken at a birthday party in 1998: on-camera flash, warm faded tones, white instant-print border, joyful candid energy, subtle grain and light leaks.

Step by step

  1. Pick a clear photo. Faces in good light work best — the AI keeps what it can see.
  2. Open your AI image app and upload the photo.
  3. Paste a prompt from above and generate.
  4. Not right? Generate again, or add one line: “make the flash stronger” / “more faded colors”.
  5. Save in full quality and post — the white border is part of the look, don't crop it off.

Tips that make it look real

  • Group photos and party shots suit this style more than formal portraits.
  • If faces change too much, add “keep faces 100% identical” to the prompt.
  • Slightly messy, candid photos come out more believable than posed ones.
